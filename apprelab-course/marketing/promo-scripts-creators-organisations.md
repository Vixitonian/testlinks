# Apprelab promo scripts: creators and organisations

Pace 2.4 words per second. Voice: Supabein, en-NG-EzinneNeural. Bracket tags are not spoken. Messaging from `apprelab-positioning.md`.

## Creators: "Stop teaching in WhatsApp groups" (1 minute)

**Audience:** creators already selling classes on WhatsApp. **Promise:** turn what you know into a course that sells, and keep 85%. **Character:** Kemi (an example, not a real person; "Picture Kemi" signals it). **Call to action:** Become a mentor.

[HOOK]
Abeg, resend the link. That's the fortieth message today. Picture Kemi. She teaches bookkeeping in a WhatsApp group, and honestly, she's tired.

[TENSION]
Payments arrive as screenshots she has to check one by one. Half the class never does the work. And nobody can prove what they learned. Sound familiar?

[TURN]
Same students. Less stress. Real proof. That's what happens when your class becomes a proper course.

[BUILD]
On Apprelab, a free course shows you how to plan it. The AI drafts lessons from your notes. And every lesson ends with a task, marked in seconds.

[PROOF]
Learners pay in Naira through Paystack, and you keep eighty-five percent of every sale. When they finish, they share a verified badge that points back to your course.

[ACTION]
Stop teaching in WhatsApp groups. Start selling a real course. Go to Apprelab.com and tap Become a mentor.

## Organisations: "Training you can prove" (1 minute)

**Audience:** schools, NGOs and programmes. **Promise:** courses, bootcamps and reports in one place. **Character:** Mrs Adeyemi (an example, not a real person). **Call to action:** Partner with us.

[HOOK]
Your trainees attended. But did they learn? Picture Mrs Adeyemi. She runs a skills programme for two hundred young people in Ibadan.

[TENSION]
She has attendance sheets, a noisy WhatsApp group, and a sponsor asking for results. Her reports take a whole weekend, and they still can't show who can do the work.

[TURN]
Here's the shift. Attendance isn't proof. Finished work is. And now you can see it.

[BUILD]
On Apprelab, she invites her whole cohort at once. They learn in short lessons on their phones, and every lesson ends with a real task, checked in seconds.

[PROOF]
Her bootcamp runs in one place: live classes with reminders, group chats, and a report for every person and every course. Learners who pass every task earn a verified badge.

[ACTION]
Training you can prove. Go to Apprelab.com and tap Partner with us.
